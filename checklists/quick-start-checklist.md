# Civic Resilience: Quick-Start Action Checklist

> **About This Checklist:** A practical, printable reference guide bringing together the core defensive actions, digital safety protocols, and physical survival steps from all 4 pillars of the *Dutch Civil Preparedness & Digital Resilience Framework*.

---

## Pillar 1: Mobile Security & Advanced Spyware Defense

### Daily Habits & Memory Flushing
- [ ] **Perform a Daily Phone Restart:** Completely turn off your smartphone for 30 seconds once every 24 hours to clear short-term memory (RAM) and flush non-persistent zero-click exploit payloads.
- [ ] **Install Updates Immediately:** Enable automatic operating system (iOS/Android) and app updates to patch critical background security vulnerabilities as soon as fixes are released.

### Device Hardening
- [ ] **Enable Lockdown Mode (iOS users):** Go to *Settings > Privacy & Security > Lockdown Mode* and tap *Turn On Lockdown Mode*.
- [ ] **Enforce Security Settings (Android users):**
  - Disable *Install Unknown Apps* (*Settings > Security & Privacy > Install Unknown Apps*).
  - Enable *Auto Restart* (*Settings > Device Care > Auto Optimization*).
  - Turn on *Lockdown Option* (*Settings > Lock Screen > Secure Lock Settings*).
- [ ] **Disable Unused Connections:** Turn off Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and NFC in public spaces when not actively using them.

### Wireless & Signal Safety
- [ ] **Disable Auto Wi-Fi Connect:** Set your device to never automatically connect to open or public Wi-Fi networks.
- [ ] **Report Illegal Jamming:** If experiencing sudden, total localized wireless blackout without a grid power outage, report radio interference directly to the **Rijksinspectie Digitale Infrastructuur (RDI)**.

## Pillar 2: AI Fraud, Voice Cloning & Digital Footprint Safety

### Family Emergency Protections
- [ ] **Establish a Family Safe Word:** Agree on a secret, un-guessable code phrase with family members. Ask for this safe word if anyone calls claiming to be in an emergency or requesting urgent money.
- [ ] **Practice "Hang Up and Call Back":** If a relative, bank officer, or police officer calls unexpectedly asking for money or credentials, immediately hang up and call them back on a known, verified phone number.

### Digital Footprint Hygiene
- [ ] **Set Profiles to Private:** Restrict access to personal social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn) to limit public audio/photo harvesting.
- [ ] **Audit Data Breaches:** Check primary email addresses on open-source breach trackers like **[Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com)**.
- [ ] **Rotate Reused Passwords:** Use an open-source password manager (e.g., Bitwarden or KeePassXC) to generate unique passwords for every online account.
- [ ] **Upgrade to App-Based MFA:** Replace SMS verification codes with time-based authenticator apps (e.g., Aegis, Ente Auth, or Bitwarden Authenticator).

## Pillar 3: Physical & Emergency Preparedness (72-Hour Kit)

### Water & Food Supplies
- [ ] **Store Emergency Water:** Keep a minimum of **3 liters of drinking water per person per day** (9 liters per person for a 3-day outage).
- [ ] **Stock Non-Perishable Food:** Store 3 days of ready-to-eat food that does not require refrigeration or cooking (canned goods, energy bars, peanut butter, dried fruit).
- [ ] **Pack a Manual Can Opener:** Ensure a non-electric hand can opener is stored directly with your food supply.

### Light, Power & Communication
- [ ] **Store LED Lighting:** Keep battery-powered flashlights or headlamps easily accessible (avoid using candles due to fire hazards).
- [ ] **Keep Power Banks Charged:** Maintain at least two 10,000–20,000 mAh USB power banks reserved for phone charging and emergency lighting.
- [ ] **Secure an Emergency Radio:** Keep a battery-powered or hand-crank emergency AM/FM/DAB+ radio stored with fresh spare batteries.
- [ ] **Print Essential Contacts:** Maintain a physical paper list of essential phone numbers (family, doctor, non-emergency police **0900-8844**) and a designated family meeting location.

### Financial & Heating Safety
- [ ] **Keep Physical Cash:** Store €100–€200 in small notes (€5, €10, €20) and coins for cash-only purchases during payment grid blackouts.
- [ ] **Install Carbon Monoxide Detectors:** Ensure battery-operated CO detectors are installed. *Never use outdoor camping stoves or gas heaters indoors.*

## Pillar 4: Open Knowledge & Community Cooperation

### Neighborhood Preparedness
- [ ] **Connect with Neighbors:** Identify elderly or vulnerable neighbors who may need assistance during blackouts or severe winter weather.
- [ ] **Establish Local Meeting Spots:** Agree on a central physical meeting location in your building or street if mobile networks fail entirely.
- [ ] **Share Knowledge:** Share plain-language safety guides and family safe word strategies with relatives, friends, and community groups.

### Open-Source Participation
- [ ] **Report Threats or Ideas:** Open an **Issue** on the project's GitHub repository to alert others about new phone scams or safety concerns.
- [ ] **Contribute Edits or Translations:** Submit a **Pull Request** on GitHub to improve guidelines or translate documents into local community languages.
